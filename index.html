    TRUTH & LIMITS

    Clear answers, no hidden findings.

    What we cannot verify matters as much as what we can.

    Does entering a URL fetch my store?

    No. The first URL-format action is local. Only when approved-source checking is actually enabled will a separate, explicit action read one public page. Neither action accesses your Merchant Center account. Open the free comparison checker to work with your own product observations.

    Can you see my Google Merchant Center issues?

    Not from a public product URL. Merchant Center details require appropriate authorized account data.

    What if no discrepancy is found?

    We report the checks actually performed and the limits. No discrepancy observed in a bounded check is not proof that the whole store is problem-free.

    Do you change prices or product feeds?

    No. PriceBriefs helps document evidence and next steps; you retain control of your store.

    What do I pay?

    The current public five-SKU audit has an indicative price of US$349 excl. applicable taxes. A written quote confirms the agreed currency, scope, price and reviewer before work.
