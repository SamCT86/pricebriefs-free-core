    QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

    Know exactly what you get.

    Choose the free checker or an audit with a clear scope.

    Does entering a URL fetch my store?

    The free URL-format check runs on your device. It does not fetch the page or access your Merchant Center account. Open the free comparison checker to work with your own product observations.

    Can you see my Google Merchant Center issues?

    Use your Merchant Center diagnostics for account-specific issues. A public product URL alone cannot establish them.

    What if no discrepancy is found?

    You get the result for the checks performed, including what was covered. A check of selected products does not establish that the entire store is issue-free.

    Do you change prices or product feeds?

    You stay in control. PriceBriefs documents evidence and next steps; it never changes your prices or feeds.

    What do I pay?

    The free checker costs nothing. A focused audit of one pricing or product-data issue has an indicative price of US$349 excl. applicable taxes. Your written quote confirms the scope, reviewer, price, currency and taxes before work.
