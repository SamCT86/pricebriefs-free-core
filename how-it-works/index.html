THE METHOD

Four steps, in order.

Each step removes one way a price difference can mislead you before you act on it.

01

Enter the product details.

Enter or paste a CSV with product, brand, pack, condition, competitor source URL, prices and observation time. Use only information you are entitled to use.

02

Match the exact offer.

Use the same manufacturer-issued identifier on both listings (MPN or GTIN), plus brand, type, variant and pack. Different shop-internal SKUs are not proof of a match. If a common identifier is missing, the checker says HOLD.

03

Flag missing or stale details.

Unconfirmed stock, an invalid source URL or price, or an observation older than 24 hours triggers HOLD.

04

Confirm the offer before acting.

Compare the numbers, verify the retailer's page yourself and export your local findings. Pricing changes remain your decision.
