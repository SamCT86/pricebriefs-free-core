What this service is

PriceBriefs is operated by Sarmad Tawfeek (individual; no company yet) and is a free, browser-local decision aid. It checks whether the fields you enter for two product observations look comparable and returns REVIEW or HOLD. It does not fetch retailer pages and does not verify market prices.

No price guarantee or recommendation

REVIEW means only that the entered fields appear comparable. It is not a pricing recommendation, not verified competitor data and not a promise about stock, tax, shipping or identity. HOLD is a valid and useful outcome.
