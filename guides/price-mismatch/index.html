PRICE MISMATCH / PRACTICAL GUIDE

Feed price and product page disagree. Where do you start?

Start by checking whether the feed and product page describe the same offer. A public price difference alone does not establish a Google Merchant Center rejection.

Open the free comparison checkerRequest an audit

CHECK THE FACTS

Six details to check first.

  1. Manufacturer product ID, model and variant.
  2. Single unit versus multipack or bundle.
  3. Market, currency and taxes.
  4. Stock and condition of the offer.
  5. Campaign or member pricing versus the visible base price.
  6. When each source was observed and whether it is current.

CHECK YOUR ACCOUNT

Confirm the issue in Merchant Center.

A product page alone does not reveal your submitted merchant feed, processing errors, policy settings or advertising eligibility.

Know what to check next. If feed or account diagnostics are missing, mark the account status UNKNOWN and gather the missing evidence.

Use the diagnostics in your own merchant account to confirm the actual issue and affected items.
