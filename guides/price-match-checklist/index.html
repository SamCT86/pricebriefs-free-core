Five checks before matching a competitor price

A cheaper listing is only useful when it is the same offer. Use these five checks before a promotion, price change or catalog release.

1. Confirm product identity

Check manufacturer, model, GTIN or MPN and exact variant. Do not assume store-specific SKU codes identify the same product.

2. Match quantity and condition

Compare pack size, unit quantity, bundles, refurbished versus new, and included accessories. If quantity differs, hold the direct price comparison.

Sample prices · six-copy photo montage.

3. Confirm the offer is available

Record market, stock status and observation time. Use a current, available offer before making a pricing decision.

4. Compare the terms

Check currency, tax treatment, shipping, membership and promotional conditions. Treat missing information as unknown, not zero.

5. Record the evidence and assign the fix

Record public source URLs, timestamps, the observed mismatch, the proposed correction and an owner. Recheck after correction. Separate measured facts from estimated financial exposure.

Estimate the exposure

If you reduce price by 20 per unit and sell 500 units at the same volume and cost, gross profit falls by 10,000. This example uses assumed figures, not a customer result. Demand, fees and tax may change actual outcomes.
