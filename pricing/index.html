Scope and terms of the paid audit

One problem class, one market and currency, up to five selected public SKU variants. We check source URL and UTC evidence, identity, variants, negative controls, currency, stock and links where verifiable, and identify a fix owner. Qualified reviewer, contracting seller/payee, delivery cost, taxes and acceptance criteria are agreed case by case. Agency pricing is separately quoted. No logins or customer order data are needed. Nothing here creates an automatic sale or binding quote. This page cannot accept an order or payment and cannot take payment or make a binding offer.

Good to know

A few quick answers.

Does the checker fetch live prices?

No automatic retailer crawling or subscription checkout is available. The checker compares observations you enter; live monitoring is not available.

What happens to my data?

The free checker runs locally. Audit inquiries are sent separately through the request form. Read about data and trust.

When do I pay?

Only after the scope, quote and acceptance criteria have been agreed separately in writing. There is no automatic checkout.
