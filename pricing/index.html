Pricing principles

You shouldn't have to trade your data for a free tool.

01

No card required.

Using the browser preview costs nothing. No core subscription checkout is connected here.

02

Your inputs stay yours.

We do not sell your store inputs in this prototype. Any future aggregate learning would require an explicit, optional permission choice.

03

No surprise AI costs.

Hosted crawl and agent usage will need published limits, abuse prevention and measured unit costs before release.

The questions we'd ask too.

Can I sign up for live monitoring today?

No. Customer access is not open. The local review checker works with data you enter; it does not visit retailer websites.

Will my data be sold?

This browser-only checker sends no observations to PriceBriefs. Any future data-sharing program would require separate notice and explicit opt-in; there is no such program active here.

Is the free checker the paid audit?

No. The checker only compares data you enter. A human-reviewed storefront audit is a separate professional deliverable discussed and quoted in writing. There is no subscription or checkout here.
