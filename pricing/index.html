Scope and terms of the paid audit

We focus on one product-data problem across up to five selected variants, in one store and market. Your audit includes dated source evidence, product and offer checks, practical recommendations and one agreed recheck. Your written quote sets out the reviewer, seller, price, taxes, payment arrangements and acceptance criteria. Agency work is quoted separately. You decide whether to proceed after reviewing the quote.

Good to know

How do I use the free checker?

Enter the product details and prices you collect, or paste a CSV. The checker helps you compare those observations. For a review of public source pages, request an audit.

What happens to my data?

The free checker runs locally. Audit inquiries are sent separately by email. Read about data and trust.

When do I pay?

We agree the scope, price and payment arrangements with you in writing before work starts.
