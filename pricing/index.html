Scope and terms of the paid audit

The standard audit covers one product-data problem, one store and market, one currency, and up to five selected public product pages. Larger investigations require a separately agreed scope. Your audit includes dated source evidence, product and offer checks, practical recommendations and one agreed recheck. Your written quote sets out the reviewer, seller, price, taxes, payment arrangements and acceptance criteria. Agency work is quoted separately. You decide whether to proceed after reviewing the quote.
