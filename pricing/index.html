Scope, availability and terms

Each proposed paid audit is for one store, market and currency. Catalog rows are supplied data, not independently verified product pages. We agree authorized sources, actual coverage, reviewer, seller, price, taxes, acceptance criteria and recheck in a written quote in an agreed currency. You decide whether to proceed after reviewing the quote. No commitment until you accept. No payment or automatic order on this site. No price changes, feed edits or promised savings.
