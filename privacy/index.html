Privacy

Free checker and audit enquiries

Version 1.1 · updated 24 November 2026.

Who is responsible

PriceBriefs is operated by Sarmad Tawfeek (individual; no company yet). Contact: hello@pricebriefs.com.

The free checker

Product details, prices, CSV input and JSON exports are processed locally in your browser and are not sent to PriceBriefs. The checker does not transmit entered observations or exports to analytics, and does not require an account or payment.

Hosting logs

Like any website, the hosting provider (Cloudflare) processes technical request data (IP address, time, URL, user agent) for delivery, security and abuse prevention. Those logs are configured by the hosting provider and are not used to profile what you typed into the checker.

Audit enquiries

The request page can prepare an email locally. Nothing is submitted automatically. If you send the email to hello@pricebriefs.com, your email address and the information you choose to include are processed by our email services, including Simply.com forwarding and Gmail, to assess and respond to your request. Do not include confidential or customer information. Previous enquiries may have used Web3Forms.

Payments and partners

You may request a non-binding quote for a human-reviewed audit. There is no payment or checkout on this website. The contracting party, price and payment arrangements are agreed separately in writing before any paid work.

What we do not do

  • The free checker does not transmit the product details or exports you enter.
  • We do not sell checker inputs. Audit enquiries use the submission and email services described above.
  • Cloudflare Web Analytics is configured for this site. Site-level measurement is separate from checker inputs; we do not use it to record the details you enter into the checker.
  • We do not change prices on your behalf.

Your rights

Where personal data is processed (hosting logs, email you send us), you have GDPR rights: access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability. Contact us at the email above. You may also complain to IMY (Swedish data protection authority).

Retention

Checker inputs are processed locally. Enquiries are retained as needed to handle the request and applicable obligations. Contact us with questions about retention or deletion. Hosting-log retention follows the hosting provider's settings.

Changes

This page is versioned; the version and date change when practices change.

Plain-language notice for the free checker and audit enquiry route. Detailed processor and retention arrangements remain subject to review before a paid engagement.
