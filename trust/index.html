No funny business

Your products.
Your call.

The checker runs on your device and does not transmit your product details to PriceBriefs. We don't quietly turn them into a data business. If we can't verify a price, we won't pretend we did.

01

No sneaky tracking.

No third-party analytics scripts, tracking pixels or external AI calls are included in this preview.

02

No data-for-free trick.

The free checker does not upload your observations. If future aggregated learning is offered, it must be separately explained and genuinely optional.

03

You're in the driver's seat.

The product will never present a user-entered source URL as independently verified proof or silently reprice a store.

Two different data paths

Product details you type in the checker stay in your browser. If you submit an audit inquiry, your work email, storefront details and message are sent via Web3Forms for review and reply. Share only public URLs and avoid confidential or customer data. Questions? Contact hello@pricebriefs.com. Serving the site itself may still involve hosting requests and server logs. Exact logging, retention, data-controller contact and binding terms must be checked and approved before a customer-ready release. This explanation is not a final Privacy Policy or Terms of Service.

The current checker cannot verify authenticity, VAT, shipping, promotion conditions, merchant identities or licensing of a URL. Do not use it as autonomous financial advice.

Proof over volume.

We would rather hold ten uncertain differences than recommend one wrong price move.
