Privacy and control

Your data.
Your decision.

The checker runs on your device and does not transmit your product details to PriceBriefs. You decide what to share and when to act.

IN THE BROWSER

Private comparisons by default.

The checker compares on your device. Nothing you type into it is sent to PriceBriefs for analysis.

01

Private product comparisons.

The checker does not send entered product observations to analytics or external AI services. We do not run our own server-side pageview or click counter.

02

Keep your own records.

Download your comparison results directly from your browser. You choose where to keep them.

03

You control your prices.

You check the original listing and decide whether to change your price.

READING A RESULT

Three result rows and what they mean.

The checker returns REVIEW or HOLD from the details you enter. Read the signal before you use any price difference.

SignalResultWhat it means
Exact ID, category, variant; fresh, stocked, URL givenREVIEWThe entered fields line up. Confirm the original offer.
Wrong product or variantHOLDDo not use the price difference.
Old date, unknown stock or unsafe URLHOLDUpdate the missing or stale details, then check again.

Before deciding on a price, check the original offer, including taxes, delivery, promotions and seller details.

WHAT YOU SHARE

What leaves your browser, and when.

Only an audit request you send yourself carries details to our desk. You choose what it contains.

What stays private and what you share

Product details you type in the checker stay in your browser. The request page prepares an email locally; if you choose to send it, your email and the details you include go to the audit desk through our email services for review and reply. Share only public URLs and avoid confidential or customer data. Questions? Contact hello@pricebriefs.com. Serving the site itself may still involve hosting requests and server logs. See our Privacy Policy and Terms for details.

LIMITS

Four limits of a public page.

A public page shows what a shop publishes. It cannot show account state, customer impact, root cause or whole-store health.

What the page cannot tell you

  • Account state. Merchant Center diagnostics and feed uploads are only visible inside your account.
  • Customer impact. A mismatch does not prove a lost sale, a rejection or a penalty.
  • Root cause. The cause can sit in the feed, the theme, a manual edit or a third-party integration. A page cannot separate them.
  • Whole-store health. Checked products are checked products, not the catalogue.

This is why the audit is scoped, dated and written: so the line between observed and unknown is on paper and not in a guess.
