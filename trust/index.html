Privacy and control

Your products.
Your call.

The checker runs on your device and does not transmit your product details to PriceBriefs. You decide what to share and when to act.

01

Private by design.

The checker does not send entered product observations to analytics or external AI services. Cloudflare Web Analytics is configured separately for site-level measurement.

02

Your observations stay with you.

Your product observations stay in your browser.

03

You're in the driver's seat.

You check the original listing and decide whether to change your price.

What “review” does and doesn't mean.
