Privacy and control

Your products.
Your call.

The checker runs on your device and does not transmit your product details to PriceBriefs. You decide what to share and when to act.

01

Private by design.

The checker does not send entered product observations to analytics or external AI services. Cloudflare Web Analytics is configured separately for site-level measurement.

02

Your observations stay with you.

Your product observations stay in your browser.

03

You're in the driver's seat.

You check the original listing and decide whether to change your price.

What “review” does and doesn't mean.

SignalResultWhat it means
Exact ID, category, variant; fresh, stocked, URL givenREVIEWInputs are comparable on their face. Check source manually.
Wrong product or variantHOLDDo not use the price difference.
Old date, unknown stock or unsafe URLHOLDGather a better observation before deciding.
Two different data paths

Product details you type in the checker stay in your browser. The request page prepares an email locally; if you choose to send it, your email and the details you include go to the audit desk through our email services for review and reply. Share only public URLs and avoid confidential or customer data. Questions? Contact hello@pricebriefs.com. Serving the site itself may still involve hosting requests and server logs.
